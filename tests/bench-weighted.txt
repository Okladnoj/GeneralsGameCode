NOT EVERY CELL MAY BE USED. The grid is measured in full, but the
argument type and the x87 precision control are not free of one
another, and a cell where they disagree cannot carry the math of
the game: the CRC would part company with the other platforms.

x87 works in 80 bit registers and the precision control decides how
many mantissa bits each result is rounded to. To match a platform
that computes float in real float - SSE2 on x64, ARM on macOS - the
x87 has to round to 24 bits. For double it has to round to 53. A
float route under _PC_53 keeps intermediates wider than the type
asks for; a double route under _PC_24 cuts them shorter. Either way
the bits diverge.

  ok       type and precision control agree, deterministic
  no det   they disagree, results will not match other platforms
  partial  the mixed route calls both entry points, so whichever
           setting is chosen it is wrong for the other type

x64 and macOS have no x87 precision control at all: every operation
is computed at the precision it was declared with, so every route
there is sound.
